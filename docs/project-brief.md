# Project Brief

## Overview
This project is a single-page portfolio website for **Мелания Ваниосова**, a Web Designer and AI Specialist. The site aims to attract potential business clients by showcasing her expertise in web design, AI integration, and her structured working process.

## Goals
- Position Melania as a highly competent professional who combines thoughtful structure with modern visuals (including AI-generated graphics).
- Clearly explain the working process.
- Highlight key advantages (use of neural networks, rich experience, flexibility).
- Showcase a portfolio of recent case studies.
- Drive conversions through clear Calls to Action (CTA) to order a website.

## Target Audience
- Business owners, entrepreneurs, and marketing managers seeking a reliable web designer for landing pages, multi-page sites, online stores, or branding.
- Clients who value modern, unique visuals (such as AI graphics) and a well-thought-out UX/UI structure.

## First Impression
- Modern, professional, innovative, and creative. The use of vibrant colors, gradients, and unique graphic elements (implied by the PDFs) should create a strong visual impact.

## Page Structure
1. **Header**: Logo/Name, Navigation Links, CTA Button.
2. **Hero Section**: Main headline, profession/niche, sub-headline, CTA Button.
3. **Process (Этапы работы)**: Step-by-step breakdown of the design and development workflow.
4. **Advantages (Мои преимущества)**: Key selling points (Neural Networks, Experience, Flexibility).
5. **Services**: Large typography listing services (Multi-page site, Landing, Online Store, Branding).
6. **Portfolio / Cases (Кейсы)**: Showcase of past projects.
7. **Footer / Final CTA**: A strong prompt to order a website. (e.g. "ЗАКАЖИ САЙТ СЕЙЧАС").

## Key Messages
- "Проектирую сайты для бизнеса с продуманной структурой и крутым визуалом" (I design websites for business with thoughtful structure and cool visuals).
- Use of unique AI graphics instead of stock photos.
- Extensive experience with large companies (Sber, Gazprom).
- Flexibility and a balanced approach between visual ideas and business goals.
